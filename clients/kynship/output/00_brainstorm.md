## 4. Initial hypotheses for THIS client

### Lead pain hypothesis
**"You did everything right and Meta still isn't scaling — because the system around the ad account (P&L inputs, creative volume, testing velocity) is the actual bottleneck, not the buyer."** Evidence: Miranda (5-yr media buyer on cost controls, still stuck) `[CORPUS-8]`; Laura Ferber (agency improving slowly, not fast enough) `[CORPUS-9]`; Two-Period (sub-1 ROAS at $6K/wk despite spend) `[CORPUS-1]`. Kynship's own onboarding language matches this almost verbatim — the "UGC death loop" + "law of diminishing returns just won't quit." `[CLIENT-DOC-1]`

### Counter-hypothesis (steel-man)
The lead pain might actually be **trust in the agency relationship**, not Meta scaling. Multiple calls (11, 24) show prospects who are loyal to underperforming agencies and need a financial/strategic narrative to feel justified switching. If we lead with "Meta is broken for you," they'll defend their incumbent. If we lead with "your agency isn't reading your P&L," they'll feel seen. The lead pain may need to be wrapped in a *trust frame*, not a *performance frame*.

### 3 angle directions to pressure-test in DRP / Strategy
1. **"P&L → Media plan, not the other way around."** Concrete proof: 12-month projection model + cost-controls testing throughput. Targets Heads of Growth/CMOs at $2M–$30M DTC who already have a media buyer.
2. **"UGC death loop is a volume problem, not a creative problem."** Anchor on 200–300 creatives/month + testing velocity. Targets brands that admit they can't keep up with Meta's creative appetite.
3. **"Your boutique brand deserves a boutique agency — not 1 of 150."** Direct shot at CTC-tier competitors. Targets founders who've been ghosted by big-agency account managers. Use the 18–20 client cap as proof.

### 2 SMS/email opening lines borrowed from corpus to A/B against new copy
1. *"Saw your team's been on cost controls all year — most brands plateau on Meta volume around month 6. The fix isn't bidding, it's testing throughput. Worth 15 min?"* — borrowed from Miranda's actual situation `[CORPUS-8]`
2. *"BFCM 2025 vs 2026: cutting CAC is going to kill a lot of DTC brands. Want our model?"* — direct lift from Kynship's own 2nd-highest-open subject line `[CLIENT-DOC-1]`

### Objections we should pre-empt
- **Pricing.** Pre-qualify on revenue ($1.5M+ ARR floor). Lead magnet is the projection model so under-qualified prospects self-deselect.
- **"We have an agency."** Frame the audit as a *second opinion*, not a switch — "8–10 hrs of our time, less than 1 hr of yours."
- **"I need to wait for [new hire / quarter / BFCM]."** Use BFCM as the urgency anchor: any agency switch made post-July is a wasted Q4.
