## ★ EDUCATION LAYER — make july 6 a *rule*, not bare scarcity
A naked "window closes july 6" reads as manufactured urgency (salesy, ignorable). An **education play** says *what the rule is* high-level → credibility + the "huh, I didn't know that" → it's also the disarmer for "we don't do R&D / we didn't know." We can't fit the full rule in an SMS, so **plant the seed; the reply/estimate teaches the rest.** The nugget that lands: **a 2022 rule quietly made you overpay on R&D; the new law lets you claw it back — till july 6.** ("overpaid" = the load-bearing word: money that's rightfully theirs.)

> ✅ **Validated against striketax.com/obbba-rd-tax-credit-refunds:** "overpaid" is Strike's OWN word — *"you likely overpaid your taxes by tens or hundreds of thousands of dollars. Congress fixed this, and the fix is retroactive."* Use **2022–2024** (Strike leads with all 3; extended-2022 filers open to Sept/Oct 2026, and the July 6 election covers 22-24). July 6 = the **≤$31M retroactive election** cutoff (every business can still amend for the credit on the normal 3-yr window — but for our ≤$31M list, "claim your 22-24 refund before july 6" is clean + accurate).

**The swappable deadline clause (drop into any variant below) — two dials:**
