## PROOF MENU — ways to say the result

Every framing below is a reframe of the **real** $1.5M / 4.2x MER / Q4 / Meta+Amazon win. Reframing is fine; inventing is not. Note the live problem with the raw line first:

> **What to avoid:** *"added $1.5M in Q4."* The "in Q4" both **discounts** the win (peak gifting season for a dog-treat brand did the heavy lifting — and pet treats are heavily gift-driven in Q4) **and dates it stale** (Q4 is long gone now, June 2026). Drop or neutralize "Q4."

| # | Framing | Move used | Fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | "~$6M/yr in revenue" | Chunk up (×4 run-rate off the $1.5M quarter) | Founder who thinks in annual scale; the ecom-founder "chunk up" note |
| **P2** | "$1.5M in 3 months" | Drop "Q4" + chunk down to the clean window | Founder — concrete, punchy, reads recent (no stale-season baggage) |
| **P3** | "$1.5M in a single quarter" | Re-time — keep the speed signal, lose the "Q4" word | Founder — speed without naming the season that discounts it |
| **P4** | "$1.5M in their best quarter" | Re-time, honest, keeps the win, drops the gift-season tell | Founder — defensible on a call ("their best quarter" is true and impressive) |
| **P5** | "4.2x on their ad spend" | Native ecom unit / strongest efficiency currency | A *spend-conscious* founder who lives in MER/ROAS — proves it was profitable, not just topline bought |
| **P6** | "$1.5M in 3 months at 4.2x on ad spend" | Pair topline + efficiency (one strong number leading, one backing) | Founder who's been burned by agencies buying revenue at a loss — the MER is the "this was actually profitable" proof |
| **P7** | "from one quarter to a $6M/yr pace" | Chunk up as a *trajectory* (before→pace) | Founder — frames it as momentum, not a one-off |

**Notes on the spread:**
- **One number does more than two** (Voice Profile). P6 stacks topline + MER — only reach for it if the MER is doing real work (the "we didn't just torch budget" objection). Otherwise lead on a single clean figure (P1, P2, P4).
- **4.2x is a clean absolute** — no % or decimal-of-a-decimal problem. "4.2x on their ad spend" passes the closer-on-a-call test. (Avoid writing it as "420% ROAS" — that's the banned percentage form.)
- **Chunk-up honesty check:** P1/P7 annualize a *single peak quarter*. That's defensible **if** Acceler8 would stand behind "$1.5M in their best quarter is a ~$6M/yr pace" on a call. If Q4 was a true seasonal spike for a treat/gift brand and the other quarters are much smaller, the ×4 may overclaim — see Open Questions.

## MECHANISM MENU — ways to say what Acceler8 did

Literal: **"cross-channel MER optimization — coordinated Meta + Amazon to maximize Q4 blended return."** Jargon label → plain picture. Every option ships **connector-led** (the connector is part of the line). The Meta+Amazon coordination is the genuinely differentiating bit — most pet/DTC brands run those two channels in separate silos — so this is real material, not filler.

| # | Mechanism line | Pattern | Connector | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **M1** | "by getting their Meta and Amazon working together instead of fighting each other" | Contrarian | by | Names the boring default (channels run in silos / cannibalizing each other) and inverts it. A founder running both *feels* this pain. |
| **M2** | "by coordinating their Meta + Amazon so every ad dollar worked twice" | Plain-English picture | by | Makes "cross-channel" visible — the dollar doing double duty across both stores. Closest to W11's plain-picture move. |
| **M3** | "mainly by running their Meta and Amazon as one engine instead of two separate accounts" | High-level abstraction | mainly by | "mainly by" = honest partial attribution (you ran the coordination, not literally every lever). Good if claiming 100% would overclaim. |
| **M4** | "by pointing their Meta spend at the channels actually printing profit, not just the ones printing revenue" | Plain-English / contrarian | by | Surfaces the *MER* idea as a picture — scaling profitable spend, not vanity revenue. Pairs with the 4.2x proof. |
| **M5** | "by restructuring their ads around blended return across both channels" | Plain-English | by | The most literal honest translation of "MER optimization." Slightly more jargon-leaning — fine for a sophisticated founder, but M1/M2 are stickier. |
| **M6** | *(omit, lean on proof)* | — | — | Viable only if the $1.5M/4.2x + an authority marker carries alone. **Not recommended** — the Meta+Amazon coordination is the curiosity hook and the differentiator; omitting wastes the most interesting thing. |

**The contrarian beat (M1) is the strongest curiosity play** — "working together instead of fighting each other" mirrors W1's contrarian energy and names a default the buyer recognizes in themselves. **M2 is the safest plain-picture** (W11-style) if you want believability over intrigue.

**Avoid:** "cross-channel MER optimization" verbatim (jargon label, dead — exactly what we're translating away from), "blended ROAS optimization," "full-funnel omnichannel attribution" (all dashboard-speak the Voice Profile bans).

## WAYS TO COMBINE — which proof × mechanism pairings hang together

One mechanism should explain one result *at the same scale*. Pairings that don't mismatch:

- **P1/P7 ($6M/yr or "$6M/yr pace") + M1 or M2** — big topline scale + a mechanism big enough to explain it ("two channels as one engine" plausibly produces year-scale numbers). This is the **"fly high for the founder"** combo — chunked-up revenue + a sticky contrarian/picture mechanism. Mirrors W1's shape.
- **P2/P4 ($1.5M in 3 months / best quarter) + M2** — concrete window + concrete picture. The most **believable, plain** combo (W11 shape). Lowest risk.
- **P5/P6 (4.2x, or $1.5M at 4.2x) + M4** — the efficiency proof paired with the efficiency mechanism ("profit not just revenue"). Internally consistent: the MER number *is* what M4 describes. Best for the **spend-burned, profit-obsessed founder**.
- **P3 ($1.5M in a single quarter) + M1** — speed + contrarian. Punchy curiosity combo.

**Don't pair:** a chunked-up "$6M/yr" with the narrow "profit not revenue" mechanism (M4) — M4 is a *quality* claim that fits the MER framing, not the scale framing; pairing them muddies which thing the number proves.

**Variant pair worth handing to sms-draft** (mirrors the W16/W17 Kynship split):
- *Curiosity/unique variant:* P2/P4 + **M1** (contrarian, mechanism-forward).
- *Authority/proof variant:* P6 + **M2 or omit**, leaning on the revenue + MER + any Acceler8 credibility marker.

## OPEN QUESTIONS — your calls to make

1. **How aggressive do we chunk the number?** Is "$1.5M in their best quarter is a ~$6M/yr pace" (P1/P7) defensible for Wufers, or was Q4 a true seasonal spike for a gift-heavy treat brand where the other quarters are much smaller? If seasonal, I'd lean on **P2/P4** (the honest single-quarter window) over the ×4 annualization. **Your call on the run-rate's defensibility.**

2. **Topline vs efficiency as the lead?** Founders mostly count **revenue** (argues P1/P2/P4 lead, 4.2x as backup). But pet DTC is brutal on margin and these founders are often **scarred by agencies that bought revenue at a loss** — which would make **4.2x MER (P5/P6)** the more "for-me" proof. Which pain is louder in the Layer A brief for *this* segment? (I don't have the brief's pain ranking in front of me — if it flags margin/profitability pain, lead efficiency; if it flags growth/scale pain, lead topline.)

3. **Is there an authority marker on Acceler8?** W7 (ex-Google) and the Kynship/Velox winners lean hard on credibility. If a founder/operator at Acceler8 has a relevant background (ex-Meta, scaled a known pet brand, etc.), that becomes a third carrier and could let the mechanism go broader (M3/M6). If not, the mechanism (M1/M2) carries the curiosity.

4. **Contrarian vs plain — how much curiosity vs how much belief?** M1 (contrarian, "fighting each other") is the more *interesting* line; M2 (plain picture) is the more *believable* one. For a sophisticated, much-pitched ecom founder, I lean **M1 for the bite + a strong number for belief** — but if Wufers' number feels modest next to the comps (Cymbiotika $1.5M/yr, Supergut $1m/month), go **M2/plain** so nothing reads oversold.
