## Service delivery process (the literal mechanism)

**1. Discovery & Qualification**
- Onboarding call + structured intake form → capture company size, industry, employee functions, historical spend.
- Proprietary qualification matrix aligns activities against the IRS "4-Part Test" (Purpose, Technological, Uncertainty, Experimentation).

**2. Technical Deep-Dive**
- Interviews with technical staff (engineers, developers, architects, chemists).
- Job-function mapping to quantify qualified research wages **at the employee level** (not just a top-down %).
- Integrate financial data directly from payroll, GL, and project accounting systems to eliminate errors and maximize credits.

**3. Documentation & Substantiation**
- Build a **"defense file"** for every study — compliance-grade documentation: narratives, project lists, wage allocation evidence.
- Audit defense guarantee — 100% success rate defending client claims with federal and state agencies.

**4. Calculation & Filing**
- Federal & state credits via **QRE optimization process** — run alternative scenarios (ASC vs. Regular method, state-by-state optimization) to maximize benefit.
- Deliverables to CPA/tax preparer in IRS-(signature)-ready format (Form 6765 + state forms).

**5. Post-Claim Support & Monitoring**
- Monitor legislative changes (e.g., 2025 repeal of Section 174 amortization), proactively re-open opportunities (retroactive amendments, amended returns for refunds).
- Ongoing guidance so future R&D costs are tracked proactively, not retroactively.

## What makes a call unqualified / not a fit
- **No U.S.-based R&D** (e.g., software firm with 90% of engineers in India/Ukraine).
- **Too small / too few technical staff** — fewer than 5–10 technical employees; not profitable or no significant US payroll (e.g., two-person startup, mostly sales/marketing).
- **Revenue profile mismatch** — >$31M avg annual revenue misses retroactive 174 relief (2022–2024); can still claim forward but longer sales cycle, less urgency.
- **Already locked with another R&D provider** (mid-contract / highly satisfied).
- **Wrong industry / low R&D spend** — pure service firms (low-code/no-code, law, staffing, restaurants, real-estate brokerages).
- **No appetite for documentation/compliance** — won't share project/payroll data or do technical interviews.
