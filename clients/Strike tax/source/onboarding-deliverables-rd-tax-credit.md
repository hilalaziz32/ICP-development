# Strike Tax Advisory — Onboarding Deliverables (R&D Tax Credit)

Source: Scaletopia onboarding deliverables doc (internal, R&D Tax Credit service only).
Tier 2 (self-report + website summaries). Incorporates §174 repeal via OBBBA (signed
July 4, 2025). **This is the master numbers/ICP doc.**

> Context: OBBBA restores immediate expensing for domestic R&D (TY 2025+), with retroactive
> relief for small businesses (avg annual gross receipts ≤ $31M under §448(c)) for 2022–2024
> via amended returns for refunds. Larger businesses can accelerate unamortized deductions
> starting 2025 but no retroactive amendments. Foreign R&D stays amortized 15 years. Software
> development qualifies as deductible domestic R&E.

## 2. ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

- **Revenue threshold:** under **$31M** avg annual gross receipts (§448(c), aggregating related entities) → qualifies for retroactive relief 2022–2024.
- **Sweet spot:** **$20M – $200M** revenue.
- **Company size:** **under 200 employees** ideal.
- **Engineering/technical focus:** **10+ engineers/technical staff** (developers, chemists, designers, engineers, CTO, Dir Tech/Software) → higher credits.
- **Other traits:** startups or mid-sized firms in tech-heavy industries; no profitability required (credits offset payroll taxes up to $250K/yr for startups); must have U.S. R&D.
- **Titles:** CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, VP, Finance, Dir. of Finance, President, Owner, Founder/Co-Founder, Director, Partner, Board Member, Controller, Tax Director.
