# Winner Structures — the malleable skeletons

> The 17 winners broken into their **structural slots** so you can borrow a *shape*, not copy a line.
> `winners.csv` sits underneath this as the raw tagged evidence (and the "why it worked" notes).
>
> **Use it like this:** pick your lever(s) → come here → find the closest **offer/niche under that lever**
> → borrow the skeleton and swap your content into the slots. No exact match? **Mix pieces** from two
> skeletons (e.g. an SEO result slot + a scarcity T2), or build from the universal skeleton below.
> **Principles first — never paste a skeleton onto an offer it doesn't fit.** Familiarity is the start, not
> the answer.
>
> **No winner for your lever × offer?** That's a *data gap, not a dead end.* The logged winners only cover
> the offers we've run — so the strategist supplies the direction, you borrow the **posture/skeleton** from
> the closest example, build the rest from principles + the universal skeleton, and **flag it as untested.**
> Don't fake a winner that isn't there.

## The universal T1/T2 skeleton

```
T1:  [disarmer] → [identity] → [case study: named logo + result in their unit + timeframe + (optional mechanism)]
T2:  [relevance bridge to them] → (optional: authority stack / scarcity / curiosity hook) → [soft CTA] → (optional signature)
```
- **Never-cut spine:** disarmer + identity + case study + relevance + soft CTA. Everything in brackets is
  situational — high-leverage when it fits, cut when it doesn't.
- Identity can live at the top of T1 *or* as a `– Name, Co` signature at the bottom of T2 (move it down when
  T1 is bulky). T2 sends ~5s after T1, same thread.

## FOMO winners — "they're missing out on something"

### W6 · agency lead-gen · marketing agencies · *carries:* case study + Clay specificity (no mechanism)
- **T1:** [disarmer: discovery source — "found {{company}} on {{directory}}, bit of a weird situation"] → [identity] → [case: "we helped {{case_client}} ({{credibility}}) sign {{n}} {{ICP}} in {{timeframe}} **but they're maxed for capacity**"]
- **T2:** [relevance: "saw your work with {{their_client}}, thought you could get similar results"] → [soft CTA: "interested to see how it works?"] → [signature]
- **Reach for it when:** no mechanism, but you have a scarcity/capacity reframe + Clay specificity about them.

### W12 · full-service lead-gen (guarantee) · local / vet · *carries:* the guarantee
- **T1:** [disarmer: "not sure if you're looking for more patients right now, but"] → [identity] → [offer-frame: "looking for {{n}} {{niche}} in {{city}} to pilot our {{program}} — **{{guarantee}} or you don't pay**"]
- **T2:** [case: "same system that brought {{case_client}} {{result}} in {{timeframe}}"] → [soft CTA: "lmk if I could give you a ring this week?"]
- **Reach for it when:** low-sophistication local buyer where removing the risk *is* the pitch. Mechanism omitted.

### W14 · local SEO · law firms · *carries:* case study in their language + scarcity
- **T1:** [disarmer: "a bit random, but"] → [identity] → [case: "recently helped {{case_client}} bring in {{$X in their-unit billables}} in {{timeframe}} — mostly by {{plain mechanism: ranking them #1 for '{{keyword}}'}}"]
- **T2:** [relevance: "I could get {{company}} more {{their_case_type}} the same way"] → [scarcity: "but we only work with {{n}} firms per city"] → [soft CTA]
- **Reach for it when:** the case study speaks their exact world (use their jargon for the outcome) + a per-city scarcity is true.

### W15 · outbound lead-gen · ecom marketing agencies · *carries:* logo stack + curiosity hook (no mechanism)
- **T1:** [identity + curiosity hook: "you don't know me but **I think I cracked the code to {{hard thing}}**"]
- **T2:** [case with logos: "helped {{case_client}} {{result}} — including {{logo}}, {{logo}}, {{logo}}"] → [scarcity: "looking for another {{niche}} to trial this with"] → [soft CTA: "could I show you how?"]
- **Reach for it when:** the proof is recognizable *logos*; the "cracked the code" loop opens FOMO+curiosity. (Avoid "drop you an email" — prefer "show you how.")

### W17 · Meta / creator seeding · DTC health-wellness · *carries:* household-name proof + broad mechanism
- **T1:** [identity + curiosity/FOMO hook: "you don't know me but I think I cracked the code to {{hard thing}}"]
- **T2:** [case: "took {{case_client}} {{result}} in {{timeframe}} using {{broad mechanism}}"] → [authority stack: "did this for {{household_name}} & {{household_name}} too"] → [soft CTA: "could I show u how it works?"]
- **Reach for it when:** S-tier case + famous logos. Mechanism kept *broad* on purpose (the concrete version won't fit). Fly high.

## Unique winners — "wait, that's different"

### W1 · Meta/paid creative · DTC supplements · *carries:* unorthodox mechanism + recognizable case
- **T1:** [disarmer: "I know this is random but"] → [identity] → [case: "I took {{case_client}} from {{X}} to {{Y}} in {{timeframe}} **using {{genuinely weird mechanism}}**"]
- **T2:** [weird-approach disarm: "it's a bit of a weird approach 😂 but I think it could work for {{company}}"] → [soft CTA]
- **Reach for it when:** the mechanism is genuinely unorthodox (passes "is it actually weird?"). The 😂 only earns its place here.

### W16 · Meta / creator seeding · DTC health-wellness · *carries:* mechanism-forward + case
- **T1:** [identity + hook: "you don't know me but I think I cracked the code to {{hard thing}}"]
- **T2:** [case + concrete mechanism: "took {{case_client}} {{result}} in {{timeframe}} using {{concrete mechanism}}"] → [relevance: "think it'd work for {{company}}"] → [soft CTA]
- **Reach for it when:** the concrete mechanism is strong enough to lead (vs W17's broad version). Variant pair with W17.

### W7 · paid ads (Google) · B2B commerce · *carries:* authority (credential), NOT the mechanism
- **T1:** [identity + credential: "it's {{name}} over at {{co}}. **I used to {{credential}}**"] → [case: "just helped {{case_client}} add {{result}} in {{timeframe}} without {{assumed-required thing}}"]
- **T2:** [relevance: "had a few ideas for {{company}} with {{their_category}}"] → [soft CTA]
- **Reach for it when:** a real credential is the reason to believe. (The mechanism here is weak — lead the credential, don't copy the mechanism.)

## Curious winners — "huh, how did that happen?"

### W2 · PR / authority-building · B2B tech / defense · *carries:* the case study (big name)
- **T1:** [disarmer: "feel free to ignore if {{their situation}}, but"] → [case: "I helped {{case_client}} go from {{before}} to {{after}} — mostly by {{high-level mechanism}} ({{proof beats: press, raise}})"]
- **T2:** [relevance: "saw what you're doing with {{their_thing}}, had ideas on how you'd get that same attention"] → [soft CTA: "can we chat? happy to walk you through how we did it"] → [signature]
- **Reach for it when:** the named case carries; the result is intriguing enough to open the loop. ⚠ Trim — this one runs long.

### W9 · SEO / organic · DTC health · *carries:* the case study
- **T1:** [disarmer: "this might sound random, **but hear me out**"] → [identity] → [case: "I helped {{case_client}} add {{$X organic revenue}} in {{timeframe}} — mainly by ranking them first for terms like {{keyword}} **without spending on ads**"]
- **T2:** [soft CTA: "can I show you how {{company}} can do the same?"]
- **Reach for it when:** SEO/organic offer; the result is the bite; `{{keyword}}` Clay var adds relevance. Tight T2.

### W11 · Amazon ads · DTC supplements · *carries:* the case study (mechanism is plain)
- **T1:** [identity] → [case: "we helped {{case_client}} grow {{channel}} revenue from {{X}} to {{Y}} in {{timeframe}} — using {{plain mechanism}}"]
- **T2:** [relevance: "had a few ideas for {{company}}"] → [soft CTA: "can we chat? (happy to share our full {{channel}} strategy)"]
- **Reach for it when:** solid case, ordinary-but-clear mechanism; curiosity rides on the result.

## Timely winners — "relevant right now"

### W3 · paid ads · home services / restoration · *carries:* case + trigger specificity
- **T1:** [disarmer: "bit random but"] → [identity] → [trigger: "we run ads **at the exact moment** {{trigger event in their world}}"]
- **T2:** [case: "it helped {{case_client}} add {{result}} in {{timeframe}} this way"] → [soft CTA: "can I show you how?"]
- **Reach for it when:** the value is the *moment* you act; name the trigger in their literal language.

### W13 · outbound / lead-gen · agencies / B2B · *carries:* ICP-signal specificity + case
- **T1:** [disarmer: "been looking at {{company}} and had an idea"] → [case: "we helped {{case_client}} {{result}} in {{timeframe}} with {{named method}}"] → [relevance: "think it'd work for you too"]
- **T2:** [ICP-signal: "I'd go after {{Clay signal: companies that recently {{trigger}}}}"] → [soft CTA: "could I show u how?"]
- **Reach for it when:** you have a sharp Clay ICP-signal — that specificity carries, not the named method.

## Helpful winners — "this person isn't pushing me"

> Heads-up: every *logged* Helpful winner here is SEO — that's the **data we have, not a rule.** The Helpful
> **posture is offer-blind**: "not asking for your business, but could I show you how {{company}} could do
> this in {{their space}}?", a free audit/idea give (e.g. the Accelerate "not trying to win your business,
> just an audit" play), or a capacity question. For a non-SEO offer, borrow the posture + a value-give and
> flag it untested — the strategist drives until we log a winner.

### W4 · local SEO · therapists · *carries:* case + capacity reframe
- **T1:** [identity] → [capacity question: "could {{company}} handle {{n extra units}} a week right now?"]
- **T2:** [case: "just helped {{case_client}} in {{city}} do that by ranking them #1 for {{service}} — now {{result}}"] → [relevance: "had a few ideas for you with {{service}}"] → [soft CTA]
- **Reach for it when:** the capacity question flips a pitch into a question they want to answer.

### W5 · SEO + market-expansion · roofing · *carries:* case + ideal-outcome specificity
- **T1:** [disarmer: "know this is random but"] → [identity] → [ideal-outcome: "if you're mostly doing {{current}}, I can help you break into {{the growth they want}}"]
- **T2:** [case: "did this for {{case_client}} — {{result}} after ranking #1 for '{{keyword}}'"] → [soft CTA: "quick call where I can share the strategy?"]
- **Reach for it when:** you can name the exact growth they want; the case proves the leap.

### W8 · SEO / organic · DTC regulated · *carries:* case + authority stack
- **T1:** [disarmer: "feel free to ignore if you've cracked {{outcome}} without ads, but"] → [case: "I helped {{case_client}} reach #1 for {{n}} high-intent keywords — {{X}} to {{Y}}/month in {{timeframe}}"]
- **T2:** [relevance: "saw {{company}} and had some ideas"] → [authority stack: "(also done this for {{brand}} & {{brand}})"] → [soft CTA] → [signature]
- **Reach for it when:** SEO result + you can stack recognizable names in their niche.

### W10 · local SEO · insurance · *carries:* case + baked-in relevance
- **T1:** [disarmer/capacity: "not sure if you're at capacity, but"] → [case: "I helped {{case_client}} hit #1 for {{service}} in {{city}}"]
- **T2:** [result + negation: "now writing {{n}} new {{policies}}/month without {{the thing they assume is required}}"] → [relevance: "could do something similar for you in {{city}}"] → [soft CTA]
- **Reach for it when:** hyper-local case; relevance comes free from the city/service match.
