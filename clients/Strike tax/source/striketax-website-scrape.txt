PAGE 2 — R&D TAX CREDIT ELIGIBILITY  (https://www.striketax.com/r-d-tax-credit-eligibility)
================================================================================

WHO QUALIFIES: "Any U.S. business that invests in developing new or improved products,
processes, software, or techniques may qualify for the R&D tax credit under IRC Section 41."
Applies to: C-Corps, S-Corps, Partnerships & LLCs, Sole Proprietors, Foreign Companies
(with U.S. operations), Startups & Pre-Revenue Companies.
"The credit applies to qualified research activities performed within the United States or
its territories."

--- THE IRS FOUR-PART TEST (all four must be satisfied) ---
1. PERMITTED PURPOSE: "The research must aim to create a new or improved business component:
   a product, process, formula, invention, technique, or piece of software." Goal = improve
   performance, reliability, quality, or function. "Cosmetic or style changes alone do not
   qualify."
2. TECHNOLOGICAL IN NATURE: "The activity must rely fundamentally on principles of
   engineering, computer science, biological science, or physical science."
3. ELIMINATION OF UNCERTAINTY: "At the outset...there must be uncertainty about the
   capability or method for developing or improving the product, or the appropriate design."
   KEY: "the uncertainty only needs to be new to your company, not new to the industry."
4. PROCESS OF EXPERIMENTATION: "evaluate one or more alternatives through modeling,
   simulation, systematic trial and error, or other methods to resolve the technical
   uncertainty."
SUCCESS NOT REQUIRED: "Failed experiments, abandoned prototypes, and shelved projects can
   all generate R&D tax credits."

--- QRES ---
INCLUDE:
- Employee Wages (W-2): wages to employees who directly perform, supervise, or support
  qualified research.
- Supplies & Materials: tangible supplies used and consumed during research.
- Contract Research (65%): payments to third-party contractors qualify at 65% of amount paid.
- Cloud Computing & Software: cloud costs directly tied to qualified research.
EXCLUDE: market research/surveys; QC testing after production; management/efficiency studies;
  routine data collection; adaptation for a specific customer w/o technical uncertainty;
  research conducted outside the U.S.

--- THRESHOLDS ---
Startups & Pre-Revenue: gross receipts under $5M; in business <5 years; "can offset up to
  $500,000 in payroll taxes annually."
Profitability: "You do not need to be profitable, and you can qualify even if the research
  was unsuccessful."

PAGE 3 — OBBBA R&D TAX CREDIT REFUNDS  (https://www.striketax.com/obbba-rd-tax-credit-refunds)
================================================================================

WHAT CHANGED: OBBBA (Public Law 119-21, signed July 4, 2025) created Section 174A —
"permanently restores immediate expensing for domestic R&D costs starting in 2025."

SMALL BUSINESS ($31M or less):
- Retroactively removes the amortization requirement for 2022–2024.
- Domestic R&D expenses "fully deductible in the year incurred."
- Those who did not amortize are "retroactively compliant for 2022-2024. No penalties."

LARGE BUSINESS (over $31M):
- Cannot retroactively elect expensing for 2022–2024.
- May "accelerate unamortized costs into 2025/2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-28)."

FOREIGN R&D: "Remain subject to 15-year amortization for all businesses."

THE PROBLEM PRE-OBBBA: 2017 TCJA "forced businesses to capitalize and amortize domestic R&D
expenses over five years under Section 174." Companies "either stopped claiming R&D credits
entirely or significantly reduced their claims for 2022, 2023, and 2024."

--- REFUND OPPORTUNITY ---
Federal credit rate: "6–8% of qualified research expenditures"
State credits: "5–30% range, approximately 40 states offer credits"
Typical recovery: "$50,000 to $500,000 or more from 2022-2024 amendments"
"Cash refunds from the IRS. This is not a future offset."

--- CRITICAL DEADLINES ---
OBBBA ELECTION DEADLINE: "Earlier of July 6, 2026 or 3-year deadline"
(NOTE: page heading showed live countdown "18 days left" when scraped; "If you have not

--- $31M THRESHOLD CLARIFICATION ---
"The threshold determines who gets the retroactive expensing election, not who can amend and
claim credits." ALL businesses can amend regardless of size; the threshold concerns only who
qualifies for Section 174A retroactive relief for 2022–2024. Aggregation: all members of a
controlled group / affiliated service group / common control treated as a single employer.

PAGE 6 — OBBBA JOURNAL ARTICLE
(https://www.striketax.com/journal/house-passes-obbba-reinstates-immediate-r-d-expensing)
================================================================================
OBBBA signed July 4, 2025 → new IRC Section 174A. "Domestic R&D expenses are fully deductible
in the year incurred for tax years beginning after December 31, 2024." Replaces 5-year
amortization that applied 2022–2024.
PERMANENCE: "Section 174A carries no sunset, so immediate domestic expensing is the standing
rule going forward."
FOREIGN R&E: unchanged — stays on 15-year amortization for all businesses.

RETROACTIVE RELIEF (small biz):
- Threshold: average annual gross receipts of $31M or less (Section 448(c) test) → apply
  174A retroactively for TYs beginning after Dec 31, 2021 (covers 2022, 2023, 2024).
- Aggregation: controlled group / affiliated service group / common control = single employer.
- DEADLINE: "the retroactive election must be made by the earlier of July 6, 2026 or the
  Section 6511 refund statute of limitations for the year."
DELIVERS: amend prior returns to deduct previously amortized domestic R&D; claim income-tax
refunds; refunds flow to retained earnings + shareholder equity (can improve valuations).

LARGER BUSINESS ($31M+): cannot amend prior years; recover unamortized domestic R&D balance
2022–2024 all at once on 2025 return OR spread evenly across 2025 and 2026.

§41 CREDIT IMPACT: higher immediate deductions raise QREs → "larger credits and additional
refund opportunities."
