## 1. The deadline — confirmed from the data (use this, not the old line)
**Confirmed (striketax.com OBBBA page + journal article I scraped):**
- OBBBA signed **July 4, 2025** → IRC **§174A**, permanent immediate expensing of domestic R&D from TY2025.
- Small businesses (**≤$31M** avg gross receipts, §448(c), entities aggregated) can elect §174A **retroactively for 2022–2024.**
- Retroactive election deadline = **earlier of July 6, 2026 OR the §6511 refund SOL for that year.**
- Published per-year amend/SOL dates: 2022 → Mar 15 / Apr 15 **2026** · 2023 → Mar/Apr 2027 · 2024 → Mar/Apr 2028.

**What that means today (2026-06-30) — corrected against striketax.com/obbba-rd-tax-credit-refunds:**
- **Use "2022–2024"** (Strike leads the whole OBBBA page with it). Non-extended 2022 pass-throughs closed in Mar 2026, **but extended-2022 filers are still open** (Strike's table: extended pass-throughs → **Sept 15, 2026**, extended C-corps → **Oct 15, 2026**), and the **July 6 election still covers 22–24.** So the 3-year frame is accurate + a bigger FOMO. (Earlier "drop 2022 / use 23-24" was over-cautious.)
- **What July 6 actually gates:** it's the **≤$31M retroactive Section 174A *expensing election*** cutoff (earlier of July 6, 2026 or the 3-yr window). Per Strike's FAQ, **every business can still amend to claim the *credit*** on the normal 3-yr window regardless of size — so for our **≤$31M** list, "claim your 22-24 refund before july 6" is clean + accurate; only mis-state risk is implying the July-6 cliff to a >$31M firm.
- **"Overpaid" is Strike's own framing:** *"you likely overpaid your taxes by tens or hundreds of thousands… Congress fixed this, and the fix is retroactive."* → that's the education-play nugget.

## 4. The universal Strike 1:1 SMS skeleton (the new spine)
```
T1: [disarmer + identity] → [AIMED relevance: the specific thing THEY build → "that's what the federal R&D credit pays on"] → [named proof: client + real $ + 22–24]
T2: [the qualifying question: "has {{company}} already claimed these for 23–24, or still on the table?"] → [deadline nudge: federal deadline july 6 on the retro years] → [value-give CTA: "happy to run a rough estimate - you only pay if the credits land?"]
```
Why this beats the old template: it **opens with a real question** (gets replies + self-segments claimed/not-claimed), leads with **aimed** relevance (not the banned "your salaries qualify…" claim-before-context), and the CTA is a **value-give**, not a meeting ask.
