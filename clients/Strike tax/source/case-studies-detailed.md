## ⚠ NAMED-CLIENT MATCHING GUARDRAIL (read before copy)
The roster clients are **real paid Strike clients**; the studies above are **real results**.
But we do **not** know that a given named client IS the company in a given study. So:

- ✅ **SAFE — pair the vertical, not the invoice:** "Strike has worked with manufacturers like
  **Atlantis Industries** (60-yr injection molder). In that space they've recovered **$300K–$657K**
  in R&D credits." Two true statements, no false welding.
- ✅ **SAFE — named client as social proof / logo only:** "clients include SecurEnds, Atlantis
  Industries, Geostructural, Grassland Oregon…" (all true — they're real clients).
- ❌ **RISKY — welding a specific $ to a specific name** ("Atlantis recovered $657K") — only if
  **Strike confirms** that named client is literally that study. Otherwise it's a fabricated claim
  about a third party's taxes. Don't ship it unconfirmed.

→ Cleanest path: ask Strike to confirm 2–3 name↔result pairs we can quote outright; use vertical-pairing
for everyone else.
